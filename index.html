    Spells and rotation

    Default: Sharpshooter + Diamond Arrows, with Ethereal Barrage and Divine Barrage on one Multi-Action Button pressed every 2 s. Since Jan 27, 2026 spells unlock for free at their level; drag the ones you use from the Spellbook to the Action Bar.

    Cooldowns

    Attack spells share a 2 s group cooldown. Heals have their own 1 s group, so you can heal and attack at the same moment. Support spells use a 2 s group, and the two stances share a 10 s cooldown.

    What scales them

    Holy spells grow with level and magic level. Ethereal spells grow with level and distance, so Sharpshooter, gear and potions boost them. Healing grows with level and magic level.

    Holy or physical?

    Undead and demons often take extra holy damage. Physical is the safe default elsewhere. A monster's weaknesses are in its Bestiary entry once you unlock stage 2.

    Most spawns physical

    Sharpshooter + Diamond Arrows. + on one button, every 2 s. Swap in when monsters are next to you.

    Undead and demons holy-weak

    Divine Defiance + the storm arrow that matches the weakness (Firestorm vs spectres, Froststorm at Gazer Spectres). Rotate Divine Barrage + Divine Caldera.

    Bosses

    Holy Flash at the start, Strong Ethereal Spear every 8 s, Divine Missile in between, Avatar of Light if the Wheel has it.

    Always running

    Intense Recovery every 60 s, Haste, Paladin Familiar, your stance.

    The full barrage rotation costs about 310 mana every 4 s (~75 mana/s). Expect a Superior Mana Potion (240–360 mana, 254 gp) about every 4 s before mana leech. Ultimate Spirit Potions (488 gp) cover HP and mana together.

on:
  schedule: [{ cron: "0 */6 * * *" }]

  snapshot:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    permissions: { contents: write }
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
      - run: |
          mkdir -p data
          curl -sf https://api.tibiadata.com/v4/character/Veritass \
            | jq -c '{t: .information.timestamp, level: .character.character.level, last_login: .character.character.last_login, deaths: [.character.deaths[] | {time, level, reason}]}' \
            >> data/veritass.jsonl
      - run: |
          git config user.name tracker-bot
          git config user.email tracker-bot@users.noreply.github.com
          git add data && (git commit -m "snapshot $(date -u +%FT%TZ)" && git push || true)
